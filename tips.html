🥋 Self-Defense Techniques

Simple moves that can save your life. Practice them.

✋

Palm Strike High

Best when attacker is close in front of you

  1. Keep fingers together, hand flat
  2. Aim for the attacker's nose or chin
  3. Strike upward with the heel of your palm
  4. Immediately step back and shout "FIRE!"
🦵

Knee Strike High

When grabbed from the front

  1. Grab attacker's shoulders or arms
  2. Pull them down while raising your knee
  3. Strike their groin or thigh hard
  4. Run to a crowded, well-lit place
👊

Elbow Strike High

Best when grabbed from behind

  1. Bend your elbow at 90°
  2. Swing it backward into their ribs or chest
  3. Use your body weight — not just arm strength
  4. Follow with a swift escape
🖐️

Wrist Release High

When your wrist is grabbed

  1. Rotate your wrist toward their thumb
  2. Pull sharply in the direction of the thumb
  3. Push their arm away with your other hand
  4. Move quickly away from them
🦶

Heel Kick High

When attacked from behind

  1. Balance on one leg
  2. Kick back hard with your heel
  3. Aim for their shin or knee
  4. Turn around and use palm strike if needed
🎯

Vulnerable Spots Learn

Where to strike for maximum impact

  1. Eyes — poke with fingers
  2. Nose — palm strike upward
  3. Groin — knee or kick
  4. Shins — kick with heel
  5. Throat — chop with side of hand
🆘

Escape & Alert Critical

Most important — get away and get help

  1. Shout "FIRE!" — gets more attention than "Help"
  2. Break free and run toward crowds
  3. Enter any open shop or public place
  4. Call 112 or 1091 immediately
  5. Trigger SafeHer SOS to alert contacts
🎒

Improvised Weapons Smart

Everyday items you can use

  1. Keys — hold between fingers, jab
  2. Umbrella — poke with the tip
  3. Bag — swing at their head
  4. Hot drink — throw at their face
  5. Pepper spray — aim for eyes (if legal)

💡 Safety Tips by Situation

🚗

Traveling

On the road, in cabs, or walking

📍
Share live location

Send your route to a trusted contact before you leave.

🚕
Verify cab details

Match the number plate with the app before getting in.

🪟
Sit near exits

In buses or trains, choose a seat close to the door.

📱
Fake call trick

Pretend you're on a call with someone waiting for you.

🌆
Avoid isolated routes

Stick to busy, well-lit streets even if longer.

🔋
Keep phone charged

Carry a power bank for longer trips.

🏠

At Home

Staying safe where you live

🚪
Lock doors always

Even for short trips to the mailbox.

👁️
Use peepholes

Never open without checking who's there.

💡
Motion lights

Install sensor lights around entrances.

📦
Delivery safety

Ask for packages to be left outside, not delivered inside.

🔑
Spare key

Give to a trusted neighbor, not under a doormat.

📹
Video doorbell

See visitors before answering.

🌐

Online & Social Media

Digital safety matters too

🔒
Private accounts

Make your social profiles private.

📍
No live location posts

Post stories only after you've left a place.

🕵️
Vet online friends

Never meet strangers alone in private places.

🔐
Strong passwords

Use unique passwords, enable 2FA everywhere.

📸
Limit personal photos

Avoid public photos of your home or workplace.

🚫
Block & report

Never engage with harassers — block and report.

💼

Workplace

Know your rights at work

📢
Speak up

Report harassment to HR immediately in writing.

🤝
Buddy system

Walk to parking or transport with a colleague.

📝
Document everything

Keep emails, messages, and dates of incidents.

⚖️
Know the law

POSH Act 2013 protects women at workplaces.

🎯 What To Do If...

Click on each situation to see step-by-step actions

🚶‍♀️

You think someone is following you

▾
  1. Cross the street and see if they follow
  2. Walk into a shop, restaurant, or crowded area
  3. Call a friend or family member — talk loudly
  4. If still followed, call 112 or your local police
  5. Trigger SafeHer SOS — your contacts get your location
  6. Never go home — go to a police station or a busy place
🚕

You feel unsafe in a cab or auto

▾
  1. Share your live location with a trusted contact
  2. Take a photo of the number plate — even if for show
  3. Pretend to call someone and say "I'll be there in 5 minutes"
  4. Ask the driver to stop at a busy place if you feel uneasy
  5. Get out — you don't owe anyone an explanation
  6. Report to the cab company and police
💬

Someone is harassing you online

▾
  1. Don't reply — any response fuels them
  2. Take screenshots of all messages
  3. Block the account on all platforms
  4. Report to the platform — most have specific options for women
  5. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
  6. Call 1930 — Cyber Crime Helpline (24/7)
👥

You're being physically grabbed

▾
  1. Shout "FIRE!" — louder attention than "Help"
  2. Use your strongest weapon — elbow, knee, heel
  3. Aim for eyes, nose, groin, or throat
  4. Break free and run toward people
  5. Enter any open shop and ask them to call police
  6. Call 112 immediately after you're safe
🏠

You're in an unsafe home environment

▾
  1. Call 181 — Women Helpline (24/7, free)
  2. Reach out to a trusted friend or family member
  3. Keep important documents and money handy
  4. Have a "go bag" with essentials ready
  5. Identify a safe place — a friend's home, shelter, or relative
  6. File a complaint if you're ready — police must help under Domestic Violence Act
🚌

You're being harassed in public transport

▾
  1. Say loudly: "Don't touch me!" — embarrass the person
  2. Move to a different seat or coach (near other women if possible)
  3. Look for the emergency button in metro/train
  4. Call 1820 (Indian Railways helpline) if on a train
  5. Note down time, coach number, and description
  6. Report to the police — eve-teasing is punishable under IPC 509

⚖️ Your Legal Rights (India)

Know the laws that protect you.

👮
Right to file an FIR

Police cannot refuse to file an FIR for a cognizable offense. If they do, you can send a written complaint to the Superintendent of Police under Section 154(3) CrPC.

🏠
Protection from Domestic Violence

Under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005, you have the right to protection orders, residence orders, and monetary relief.

💼
Protection at Workplace (POSH)

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2013 requires every workplace with 10+ employees to have an Internal Complaints Committee. Your complaint must be addressed within 90 days.

🚫
Zero FIR

You can file an FIR at ANY police station, regardless of jurisdiction. It will be transferred to the correct station later. Police cannot refuse this.

🎥
Right to Free Legal Aid

Women are entitled to free legal aid under the Legal Services Authorities Act. Contact your District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) or call 15100.

📱
Cyber Harassment Protection

Under IT Act 2000, Section 66E, 67, and 72, sharing private images or harassing online is a punishable offense. Report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
