🛡️ Safety Tips

📍

Share Your Location

Always share your live location with a trusted contact when travelling alone.

🧠

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, it probably is. Remove yourself from the situation.

👀

Stay Aware

Avoid distractions like headphones and phones in unfamiliar areas.

📞

Save Emergency Numbers

Keep emergency numbers on speed dial for quick access.

👭

Travel in Groups

Whenever possible, travel with friends, especially at night.

🥋

Learn Self-Defense

Basic self-defense training boosts confidence and safety.

💡

Stick to Lit Areas

Prefer well-lit, populated routes over dark shortcuts.

📱

Keep Phone Charged

Always carry a charged phone and a power bank.
