🥋 Self-Defense Basics

Simple, effective techniques every woman should know

✋

Palm Strike

  1. Keep your fingers together and hand flat
  2. Aim for the attacker's nose or chin
  3. Strike upward with the heel of your palm
  4. Immediately step back and shout for help
🦵

Knee Strike

  1. Grab the attacker's shoulders or arms
  2. Pull them down while raising your knee
  3. Strike their groin or thigh hard
  4. Run to a crowded, well-lit place
👊

Elbow Strike

  1. Keep your elbow bent at 90°
  2. Swing it backward into their ribs or chest
  3. Best used when grabbed from behind
  4. Follow with a swift escape
🖐️

Wrist Release

  1. Rotate your wrist toward the attacker's thumb
  2. Pull sharply in the direction of their thumb
  3. Use your other hand to push their arm away
  4. Move quickly away from the attacker
🎯

Target Vulnerable Spots

  1. Eyes — poke with fingers
  2. Nose — palm strike upward
  3. Groin — knee or kick
  4. Shins — kick with heel
  5. Throat — chop with side of hand
🆘

Escape & Alert

  1. Shout "FIRE!" loudly — gets more attention
  2. Break free and run toward crowds
  3. Enter any open shop or public place
  4. Call 112 or 1091 immediately
  5. Use SafeHer SOS to alert your contacts

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what you might be wondering

Is SafeHer free to use? ▾
Yes, SafeHer is 100% free. No hidden charges, no premium plans. Our mission is to make safety accessible to every woman.
Where is my data stored? ▾
All your data — contacts, alerts, profile — is stored ONLY in your browser using local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and no one else can access it.
Does SOS work without internet? ▾
The SOS button itself works offline — it saves your alert locally. However, to actually notify your contacts, you'd need internet. We recommend enabling mobile data as backup.
Can I use SafeHer on my phone? ▾
Absolutely! SafeHer is fully mobile-friendly. Just open it in your phone's browser. You can also add it to your home screen for app-like access.
