ℹ️ About SafeHer

Built with a mission to make every woman feel safe.

🎯 Our Mission

SafeHer is a free web application designed to provide women with quick access to emergency help, trusted contacts, and safety resources. In an emergency, every second counts — our goal is to make that second count for you.

✨ Features

  • 🚨 One-tap SOS with 5-second cancel window
  • 📍 Live location sharing with emergency contacts
  • 👥 Manage multiple trusted contacts
  • 📜 Complete alert history
  • 🆘 Direct dial to national helplines
  • 🌗 Dark mode & light mode
  • 💾 Works offline (data stored in your browser)
  • 📱 Mobile-friendly design

🔒 Privacy First

All your data — contacts, alerts, and profile — is stored only in your browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to any server. Your information never leaves your device.

💻 Tech Stack

Built with ❤️ using HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. Hosted for free on GitHub Pages. No frameworks, no build tools, no backend — just pure web technology.

🚨 Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, please call the national emergency number 112 or Women Helpline 181.
